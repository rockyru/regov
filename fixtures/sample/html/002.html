Business permit
How to apply for or renew a business permit, what to bring, and the fees.
How to apply

Fill in the unified form Get it at the Business One-Stop Shop or download it.
Submit your requirements Bring the documents listed below.
Pay and claim your permit Pay at the Treasurer's Office window 3. Most permits are released the same day.
What to bring

Barangay business clearance 
DTI, SEC or CDA registration 
Contract of lease or proof of ownership of the place of business 
Community Tax Certificate 
Fees
| Business | Fee |
|---|---|
| Sari-sari store | ₱500 |
| Restaurant | ₱1,500 |
| Other | Assessed at the window |

Questions

When must I renew? On or before January 20 of each year.
Can I apply online? Not yet. The form can be downloaded, but submission is in person.
