# Code of conduct

RE:GOV adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone represents the project.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what
is best for the people who use public websites. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

Many people who write in will be public servants describing their own agency's website. Treat
their work and their constraints with respect.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@regov.app**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may ban contributors,
following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
