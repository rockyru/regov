# Fix list

Spotted on the live site. Items 1 and 3 are fixed locally (not yet deployed); item 2 is the pending deploy.

1. ~~Fixed locally~~ **Preview pages overflow on phones when a title is one long unbreakable string** (seen on a DepEd rebuild: a content page titled with an email address, `…ar.llena@deped.gov.ph`). The h1, the eyebrow and the intro link push the page wider than the viewport, so the header band and rules get clipped. Fix in two places: `overflow-wrap: anywhere` / `break-words` on preview headings, eyebrows and prose links in `src/components/site/Pages.tsx` and `Blocks.tsx`, plus `overflow-x: clip` on the preview `<main>`; and have the planner reject titles that are bare emails or URLs (use the page's parent section or "Contact" instead) in `src/pipeline/plan.ts` / `understand.ts`.
2. **Deploy pending** (commits 0c2d287, f22e64e): boot reconcile, crash detection, 15-minute stall net, 20-minute watchdog, compact run strip. Ship tonight when the queue is low.
3. ~~Fixed locally~~ **Job page: live updates without the full-page reload, plus a visible loader** (feedback from a commenter on the launch post). Today the page uses `<meta http-equiv="refresh">` every 2 s, so the whole page reloads, flashes, and jumps back to the top on every update; that is the "auto scroll" people complain about. Replace it with a small client component that calls `router.refresh()` on an interval while the run is live (server components re-render in place, scroll and state are kept), and add a loader: an animated progress bar under the pipeline strip and a pulsing marker on the active step, with an elapsed timer and a "usually 4 to 8 minutes" hint. Keep the agent log newest-first (it already is); with no reload the order stops being a problem, but offer an "oldest first" toggle if asked again. Files: `src/app/jobs/[jobId]/page.tsx`, new `src/components/LiveRefresh.tsx`.
4. ~~Fixed locally~~ **Crawler gives up on bare domains with no DNS record and on dead hosts** (bir.gov.ph, fda.gov.ph, gov.ph failed with `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED`; upv.edu.ph timed out; ati2.da.gov.ph was a transient DNS miss). Now: `src/pipeline/reach.ts` tries the www./bare variant and retries a DNS miss once; a crawl that yields zero pages falls back to the Wayback Machine like a bot-blocked site. Verified on upv.edu.ph (2007 capture, 79 pages).
5. ~~Fixed locally~~ **Understand step dies with `400 Invalid request: unexpected end of hex escape`** (car.depdev.gov.ph, 29 of 30 pages done). Character-count slicing of page text can split an emoji into a lone UTF-16 surrogate; JSON.stringify emits `\ud83d` and the API's parser rejects the body. `src/lib/meta.ts` now scrubs lone surrogate escapes from every JSON body via a custom fetch. Reproduced and verified against the live API.
